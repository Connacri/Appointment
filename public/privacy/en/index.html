Privacy Policy

1. Who We Are

Appointment PMS is a hotel and resort property management application (web application and Android app, package com.planning.oran). This policy explains what the application stores, on which device or service, and how you can delete it.

Contact: Ramzi Guedouar — ramzi.guedouar@gmail.com (data protection contact).

2. Data We Store

The application is local-first: in normal use, no account and no remote service are required, and your operational data stays on your device.

  • Local operational data (default): reservations, rooms and resources, invoices, preferences, and proUSB lock data (issued card records, encoder audit log, imported CardLock.mdb snapshot). Stored in your browser's IndexedDB (database OmniBook_ObjectBox_Store) with a localStorage fallback, on the device you use.
  • Account data (only if Supabase is configured and you sign up): your email address, a password processed by Supabase (never stored in clear text by the application), your user identifier, the active session token kept by the browser, and the workspace records you create.
  • Hardware access you grant explicitly: WebHID access to the physical proUSB encoder, requested by the browser with a permission prompt, and the camera stream used to scan QR codes. QR images are processed locally and are never uploaded.
  • Preferences: selected language and display preferences, stored in localStorage on your device.
What we do not collect: no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reports or performance telemetry, no location data, no contacts, no Firebase or push notification tokens.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase (hosted PostgreSQL and authentication) — used only if the application is configured with a Supabase project and you create an account. It stores your authentication record and the workspace rows you write.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website and the application bundle over HTTPS.
  • Google Fonts — serves the application fonts (Plus Jakarta Sans, Cairo, JetBrains Mono). Requests are made to Google's font CDN.

No other third party receives data from the application. There is no analytics, advertising or marketing SDK.

4. Retention

  • Local data stays on your device until you delete it: use the in-app purge (see section 6) or clear your browser/site data.
  • Account and workspace data is retained while your account exists, then deleted through the account deletion procedure.
  • No telemetry is retained, because none is collected.

5. Security

The website and application are served over HTTPS. No signing key, server secret or credential is embedded in the application bundle. When Supabase is used, database tables are protected by row-level security and authentication is handled by Supabase. Data at rest on your device is protected by the security of your own device and browser profile.

6. Your Rights (GDPR and equivalent frameworks)

You may request:

  • access to the personal data we hold about you;
  • rectification of inaccurate data;
  • erasure of your account and associated data;
  • portability of your data in a machine-readable format;
  • objection to or restriction of processing.

How to exercise them:

  1. Local data: in the application, open the top-bar menu → Privacy & Account → Local data → Clear local data (immediate, on this device).
  2. Account and remote data: use the account deletion page, or email ramzi.guedouar@gmail.com.

Requests are answered within 48 business hours.

7. Children's Privacy

Appointment PMS is a professional tool for hospitality staff and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

Any change to this policy is published on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the application after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
