Account & Personal Data Deletion

In compliance with Google Play Store policies and GDPR / CCPA frameworks, users of Appointment PMS can permanently erase their account and the personal data associated with it. Two channels are available: the purge built into the application, and a deletion request for the remote account.

Method 1: Delete Data Stored on This Device (in-app)

Follow these steps inside the application (web or Android):
  1. Open Appointment PMS.
  2. Open the menu in the top bar (the ⋯ button at the top right).
  3. Select Privacy & Account.
  4. Open the Local data section.
  5. Press Clear local data and confirm the warning.

This immediately erases reservations, resources, invoices, preferences and proUSB lock data stored on this device. It does not touch a remote account: use Method 2 for that.

Method 2: Request Remote Account Deletion (web form)

If you no longer have access to the application, fill in the form below. It opens your email application with the request pre-filled; you press Send, and we verify the request by replying to the address you provide.

What Is Deleted, and What Is Retained

  • On your device: IndexedDB database OmniBook_ObjectBox_Store (reservations, resources, invoices, preferences), proUSB issued-card list, audit log and imported database snapshot, plus the application's localStorage keys — removed by Method 1 or by clearing site data in your browser.
  • Remote account (Supabase, when configured): your authentication record and the workspace rows you created are deleted after we verify your request from the address you provide.
  • Retention: the application collects no analytics or telemetry, so nothing else is kept. Invoices exist only as local records on your device; deleting the application data removes them.
  • Processing time: deletion requests are completed within 72 business hours after verification by email.
